Privacy Policy

Last Updated: February 12, 2025

1. Introduction

Guard my email ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our email security service and Gmail add-on.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Email Content (Temporary Processing Only)

When you scan an email using our service:

  • We temporarily process the email content (sender, subject, body, headers) to analyze phishing threats
  • This data is sent to Claude AI (Anthropic) for analysis
  • We do NOT store or retain any email content after analysis is complete
  • Email content is processed in real-time and immediately discarded

2.2 Account Information

We collect and store:

  • Email address (for account authentication and usage tracking)
  • Google account ID (for Gmail add-on integration)
  • Usage statistics (number of scans, timestamps, monthly usage)
  • Subscription tier (free or paid)
  • Subscription/payment information via Stripe (if you use paid features)
  • Stripe customer ID and subscription ID (for billing management)

2.3 Enterprise Inquiries

If you submit an enterprise demo request, we collect:

  • Company name
  • Work email address
  • Number of employees
  • Timestamp of submission

This information is used solely to respond to your inquiry.

2.4 Technical Information

  • IP address
  • Browser type and version
  • Device information
  • Error logs and performance data

3. How We Use Your Information

We use collected information to:

  • Provide phishing detection and email security analysis
  • Authenticate your account and manage subscriptions
  • Improve our service quality and detection accuracy
  • Send service updates and security notifications
  • Comply with legal obligations
  • Detect and prevent fraud or abuse

4. Data Sharing and Third Parties

4.1 Anthropic (Claude AI)

Email content is sent to Anthropic's Claude API for AI-powered phishing analysis. Anthropic's privacy policy applies to this processing: https://www.anthropic.com/privacy

4.2 We Do NOT:

  • Sell your personal information to third parties
  • Share your email content with advertisers
  • Use your emails for marketing purposes
  • Store your email content beyond the analysis session

4.3 We May Share Information With:

  • Service providers (hosting, payment processing, analytics)
  • Legal authorities (when required by law)
  • Business partners (with your explicit consent)

5. Data Retention

  • Email Content: Not stored (processed in real-time only)
  • Account Information: Retained while your account is active
  • Usage Statistics: Retained for 12 months
  • Payment Records: Retained for 7 years (legal requirement)

6. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures:

  • End-to-end encryption for data transmission
  • Secure HTTPS connections
  • Regular security audits
  • Access controls and authentication
  • SOC 2 compliant infrastructure

7. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access: Request a copy of your personal data
  • Correction: Update inaccurate information
  • Deletion: Request deletion of your account and data
  • Portability: Receive your data in a machine-readable format
  • Opt-out: Unsubscribe from marketing communications
  • Withdraw Consent: Revoke permissions at any time

9. CCPA Compliance (California Users)

California residents have the right to:

  • Know what personal information is collected
  • Know if personal information is sold or disclosed
  • Opt-out of the sale of personal information (we don't sell data)
  • Request deletion of personal information
  • Non-discrimination for exercising privacy rights

10. Children's Privacy

Our service is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13. If we discover such collection, we will delete the information immediately.

11. Gmail Add-on Specific

Our Gmail add-on:

  • Only accesses emails you explicitly choose to scan
  • Requires minimal permissions (read current message only)
  • Does not access your entire inbox
  • Does not read, store, or access emails you don't scan
  • Complies with Google's API Services User Data Policy

12. Cookies and Tracking

  • Analytics Cookies: To understand service usage (can be opted out)
  • No Advertising Cookies: We do not use cookies for advertising
