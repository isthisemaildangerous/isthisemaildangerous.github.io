🚀 How to Install Guard My Email

Installation from Google Workspace Marketplace

1

Visit the Google Workspace Marketplace

Go to the Google Workspace Marketplace and search for "Guard My Email"

Open Marketplace
2

Click "Install"

On the Guard My Email listing page, click the blue "Install" button

3

Review Permissions

You'll see a list of permissions the add-on needs:

  • Read email when you use the add-on
  • Access your email address
  • Connect to external services (Claude AI)

Click "Continue" to proceed

4

Grant Access

Choose your Gmail account and click "Allow" to grant the necessary permissions

5

Start Using Guard My Email

Installation complete! Now you can:

  • Open any email in Gmail
  • Look for the Guard My Email icon in the right sidebar
  • Click the icon to open the add-on
  • Click "Scan Now" to analyze the email

Alternative: Install from Gmail

1

Open Gmail Settings

In Gmail, click the Settings gear icon → "See all settings"

2

Go to Add-ons Tab

Click the "Add-ons" tab, then click "Get add-ons"

3

Search and Install

Search for "Guard My Email" and click "Install"

⚡ Quick Start Guide

Using the Gmail Add-on

  1. Open Gmail and select any email
  2. Find Guard My Email in the right sidebar
  3. Click the icon to open the add-on panel
  4. Click "Scan Now" to analyze the email
  5. Review results - you'll see a risk level and explanation

Using Email Forwarding

  1. Forward suspicious email to: scan@guardmyemail.com
  2. Wait 1-2 minutes for analysis
  3. Receive results via email with risk assessment

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

I installed the add-on but don't see the icon
Try these steps:
  • Refresh Gmail (press F5 or Cmd+R)
  • Check if the sidebar is collapsed - look for a small arrow
  • Try a different browser or incognito window
  • Reinstall the add-on from Workspace Marketplace
What permissions does Guard My Email need?
We only request essential permissions:
  • Read emails: Only when you click "Scan Now" - we analyze the email content
  • Your email address: To track your monthly scan quota
  • External service: To send emails to Claude AI for analysis
We never store your email content. See our Privacy Policy.
How accurate is the scam detection?
Guard My Email uses advanced AI (Claude by Anthropic) to analyze emails. While highly effective, no system is 100% accurate. Always use your own judgment and verify suspicious requests independently. See our Terms of Service for details.
I've used all my free scans. What now?
Free users get 5 scans per month. You can either:
  • Wait until next month (scans reset on the 1st)
  • Upgrade to Premium for unlimited scans at $4.99/month

Does this work on mobile?
Yes! The Gmail add-on works on:
  • Gmail mobile app (Android & iOS)
  • Gmail web on mobile browsers
  • Email forwarding works from any email client
How do I uninstall?
To remove Guard My Email:
  1. Gmail Settings → Add-ons tab
  2. Find "Guard My Email" under "Installed add-ons"
  3. Click "Manage" → "Remove"
Is my data secure?
Yes. We:
  • Never store email content
  • Use encrypted connections (HTTPS)
  • Only analyze emails you specifically choose to scan
  • Don't share your data with third parties

🔧 Troubleshooting

Common Issues and Solutions

Error: "Authorization failed"
  1. Uninstall the add-on completely
  2. Clear your browser cache
  3. Reinstall from Workspace Marketplace
  4. Grant all requested permissions
Error: "Quota exceeded"
You've used all 5 free scans this month. Either wait until next month or upgrade to Premium for unlimited scans.
Scan taking too long / timing out
  • Very large emails may take longer to analyze
  • Try refreshing and scanning again
  • If persistent, forward the email to scan@guardmyemail.com instead
Not receiving forwarded scan results
  • Check your spam folder
  • Add scan@guardmyemail.com to your contacts
  • Wait up to 5 minutes for results
  • Verify you forwarded to scan@guardmyemail.com (not a typo)
